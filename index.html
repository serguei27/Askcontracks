Common questions

Answers, straight.

What exactly is included in my plan?

Before you start, we agree in writing the contracts covered, the priority activities and the monthly capacity — including meetings and document volumes. If your needs grow, we agree additional support and price with you first.

Is there a minimum term?

No. Plans run month to month, with one month's notice.

Do you replace our lawyers?

No. We manage the contract day to day and work alongside your legal counsel. Where formal legal steps are needed, we help prepare a clear record for them.

How is this different from hiring in-house?

No recruitment and no ramp-up. You get a named senior contract manager plus access to QS, engineering and legal specialists when the scope needs them — without employing a full team.

Is this an AI tool?

No. Ask Contracks is people — a named contract manager you can call, who can join your meetings. We use software to keep registers; judgement and drafting are human.

How do you handle confidentiality?

We run a conflict check and sign a confidentiality agreement before receiving any contract documents. Documents are then exchanged through a secure channel.

Where do you work, and in which languages?

Primarily in English, French and Italian, across Europe, Africa and the Gulf. Other languages and regions are possible through partners — we confirm availability before we agree scope.

What determines the exact price?

The number of contracts, their value and complexity, the monthly capacity needed and whether a dispute is already live. You get a fixed monthly fee in writing before starting. Not ready for ongoing support? Start with a one-off Contract Review, from €500.
